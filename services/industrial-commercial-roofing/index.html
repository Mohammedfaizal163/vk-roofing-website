Material Catalog

High-Performance Roofing Sheets for Every Industry

We fabricate and supply all standard industrial roofing sheets with certified thicknesses and protective coatings.

01 / THERMAL CONTROL

Sandwich Insulated PUF Panels

Rigid polyurethane foam core (30mm, 40mm, 50mm, 100mm) sandwiched between prepainted steel sheets. Eliminates 90% of heat transfer.

02 / CORROSION RESISTANT

Color-Coated (PPGI / PPGL)

Multi-layer oven-baked paint coatings on high tensile galvalume base. Available in blue, green, terracotta red, and white.

03 / COASTAL STRENGTH

Bare Galvalume Sheets

55% Aluminium-Zinc alloy with AZ150 coating. Excellent thermal reflectivity and 550 MPa tensile strength for high-wind coastal zones.

04 / NATURAL DAYLIGHT

Polycarbonate & FRP Sheets

High light transmittance with UV stabilization. Replaces electric daytime factory lights and provides impact resistance against falling debris.

05 / SEAMLESS ARCHITECTURE

Standing Seam Metal Roofs

Continuous concealed-fastener panels without through-roof penetrations. Eliminates fastener leak points on low-slope roofs.

06 / HEAVY INDUSTRIAL

Fiber Cement & Aluminium Sheets

Corrosion-proof solutions ideal for chemical plants, fertilizer storage, salt manufacturing units, and aggressive chemical atmospheres.
