Sandwich / Insulated PUF Panels (Polyurethane Foam)

Engineered with a rigid PUF insulation core sandwiched between two galvanized/galvalume steel sheets. Delivers extraordinary thermal insulation, reducing indoor temperature by 5°C to 8°C in Tamil Nadu's scorching summers while cutting AC energy bills by up to 30%.
